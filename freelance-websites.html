Frequently asked questions

What is an all-in-one freelance website?

It is a place that brings many freelancing platforms and tools together. Job Hunt is a hub: it lists 109 freelance websites, shows live remote jobs and offers free tools. It does not host the jobs itself, so you apply on the original platform.

How many freelance websites are listed here?

This page lists 109 freelance websites and job platforms, grouped into 14 categories. The list grows as new platforms are added.

Are freelance websites free to join?

Most let you create a profile for free, but many take a fee from your earnings, and some job sites sell paid memberships. Use the platform fee calculator on the Tools page, and always read the fee page of a platform before you start.

Which freelance website should I start with?

Pick two or three that match your skill and your goal. For example, use a general marketplace to find project work, a remote job board for longer roles, and a design or tech site if you work in those fields.

How do I avoid scams on freelance websites?

Never pay to get a job, keep chats and payments inside the platform, and use the scam checker on the Learn page when an offer feels wrong.
