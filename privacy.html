Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Welcome to Job Hunt (accessible at https://mahadiahmednbj-code.github.io/job-hunt/). Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information that are collected and recorded by Job Hunt and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Job Hunt acts as a clean job search directory connecting freelancers with 100+ global job platforms. We do not require user registration, and we do not directly collect or store sensitive personal data such as passwords, payment details, or government IDs on our servers.

2. Log Files and Analytics

Like most standard websites, Job Hunt may utilize basic log files and browser analytics (such as IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider, date/time stamps, and referring pages) strictly to analyze trends, administer the site, and improve user experience.

3. Cookies and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display clean, relevant advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Job Hunt has no access to or control over cookies used by third-party advertisers.

4. Third-Party Job Platforms

When you click on job links or external platforms (such as Upwork, Fiverr, Freelancer, LinkedIn, etc.), you are directed to those third-party websites. Please review the respective Privacy Policies of those platforms, as Job Hunt is not responsible for the content or privacy practices of external sites.

5. Children's Information

Job Hunt does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through any linked service, please contact us immediately.

6. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
