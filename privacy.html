What Job Hunt does

Job Hunt is a free website that shows live job listings and links to other websites. You do not need an account.

Information stored on your device

Your browser keeps jobs you save or post, and the data you enter in the Tools, Dashboard and Learn pages (projects, goals, time logs, checklists). This stays on your device. We do not receive it. The job list is refreshed by an automatic hourly update and is loaded from this website.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Where required, a consent message is shown.

Analytics

We use Google Tag Manager to measure how visitors use the site. Tools loaded through it may use cookies. Where required, a consent message is shown.

Third-party websites

Job listings come from Jobicy, Remote OK, Arbeitnow, We Work Remotely, The Muse, Adzuna and Jooble. Apply and search buttons open other websites. Those sites have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for them.

Children

Job Hunt is not meant for children under 13.
